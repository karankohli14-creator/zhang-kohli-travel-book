K-Mate SVG Board v44 — attribution

The K-Mate full-SVG board interface was inspired by the architecture and
annotation ideas in:

  svg.chessboard.js
  https://github.com/ilhooq/svgchessboard

K-Mate's v44 implementation is new application code. It retains K-Mate's own
piece artwork, rules, move handling, touch controls, puzzle logic, and board
themes. It does not redistribute the ChessX piece or texture assets referenced
by the original project's README.
